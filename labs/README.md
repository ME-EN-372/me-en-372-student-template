# Thursday FEA labs

One memo per lab: `lab01-memo.md` … `lab14-memo.md`, started from
[`TEMPLATE-lab-memo.md`](TEMPLATE-lab-memo.md).

**Memos are individual even when the modelling was collaborative.** You may
share a model, a screen, and an argument; you write your own memo. Two identical
memos are an academic-honesty problem, not a time-saver.

**Due Thursdays at 5:00 pm Mountain Time** unless the lab sheet says otherwise.

The lab sheets themselves, with the step-by-step FEA instructions, are on Canvas
and on the course tutorial site. Several labs have a matching hands-on tutorial
you can run before the session — the lab sheet links it.
